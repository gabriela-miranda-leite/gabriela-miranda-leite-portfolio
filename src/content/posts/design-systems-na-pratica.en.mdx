When I joined Stone, the problem was classic: dozens of squads building similar components in different ways. A primary button had 7 variations.

## What changed on the second attempt

The turning point was stopping thinking about components and starting to think about decisions.

```tsx
// Decision: use `intent` instead of `variant`
type ButtonIntent = 'primary' | 'secondary' | 'danger' | 'ghost';
```

Naming the prop `intent` instead of `variant` changes the entire conversation.
